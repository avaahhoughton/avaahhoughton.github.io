An introduction

Music has always been part of my story.

I'm a singer, singing teacher and creative artist with a love of performance, connection and self-expression.

My background spans classical and contemporary singing, theatre, education and the arts. Whether I'm singing at an event, guiding someone as they discover their voice or exploring a new creative project, I'm drawn to the ways art brings people together.

Outside of music, you'll often find me enjoying nature, reading, painting or finding inspiration in everyday life.
