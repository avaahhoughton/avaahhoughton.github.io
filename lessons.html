Singing lessons

Find your voice.

Individual singing lessons with Avaah Houghton, a classically trained soprano and educator.

My approach

A little room to make music.

Whether you're singing for the first time or returning to something you've always loved, lessons are an opportunity to explore your voice at your own pace.

My teaching draws on classical vocal training, performance experience and a background in education. I aim to create a welcoming space where curiosity, technique and musical expression can grow together.

We'll work with your interests and goals, while making space for the simple pleasure of singing.

Inside a lesson

Where we begin.

There isn't one single path to finding your voice. We can shape lessons around your experience, interests and what you'd like to explore.

01

Get comfortable

Gentle warm-ups, breath awareness and exercises to help you become more familiar with your voice.

02

Build your skills

Explore pitch, tone, technique and musical confidence, with guidance suited to your stage of learning.

03

Make it yours

Bring music you enjoy, try something new and discover more freedom in how you express yourself through song.
